Game menu

The shared world does not pause.

Cross-device login. No password. Codes last 12 minutes and work once.

WASD: move. Space: jump. Ctrl: sprint. Shift: sneak.
Hold left click: mine. Left click: attack. Right click: place or use.
Hold right click with food: eat. E: inventory. Q: drop item.
1 to 9 or wheel: select. T: chat. Esc: menu.

Craft planks from a log, then a crafting table. Place and use the table for tools. A pickaxe is required for stone and ore drops. Cook food or smelt iron in a furnace using fuel. Light a shelter and survive the night.

J: exploration journal, building guides and village supply requests.
The gold bar under hunger is saturation. It drains before hunger; cooked meals last longer. Food tooltips show both values.
