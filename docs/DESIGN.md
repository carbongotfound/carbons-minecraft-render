# Visual and interaction direction

The goal is a recognizable classic voxel survival game: crisp 16×16 material
patterns, cubic clouds and opaque blue water, readable block silhouettes,
correctly proportioned animals, a central crosshair, nine inventory slots and
small unobtrusive status controls. All textures remain original artwork.

Keep the established login-code flow and account recovery. Use square stone
buttons, visible keyboard focus, pixel typography, gray inventory panels and
consistent slot borders. The world should occupy the browser viewport without
scrolling; controls must remain usable on a small laptop and on touch screens.

Acceptance checks: distinguish grass, dirt, stone, wood and ores at a glance;
stairs have stepped geometry and collision; animals animate when traveling and
show sheared coats; walking, jumping, sneaking, mining and placing work; opening
inventory releases input; account controls still work; no uncaught browser errors.

This improves the current game substantially. It does not claim Minecraft's
complete content, networking, AI, world generation or platform parity.
